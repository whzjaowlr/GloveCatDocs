# Emergency Response

Procedures for responding to security incidents.

## Emergency Contacts

- **Telegram**: @glovecatcoin
- **Twitter**: @GCATstudio

## Response Procedures

### Step 1: Pause
```solidity
pause()
```
Immediately halt all transactions.

### Step 2: Assess Situation
- Confirm scope of damage
- Analyze attack vector

### Step 3: Recovery
- Confirm whether Safe configuration, contract wiring, or liquidity operations need action
- Community announcement

## Prevention

- Regular security audits
- Real-time monitoring
